📖 Story
文法：過去形・形容詞・手紙表現

"Dear diary," wrote Yui. "I transferred to Finland today."

"Every student comes early — but school ends at 1pm!"

"The classroom is large and full of sunlight."

"It is never dark inside — even in winter."

"The homework is not hard or difficult at all."

"Poor me — I thought all schools were the same!"

"I must be careful not to be too quiet."

"Here, students talk freely and share opinions."

"It is so full of energy — so different from home."

"Dear diary — I think I love it here."

🌍 CLIL豆知識：フィンランドの教育の秘密
フィンランドはPISA（国際学習到達度調査）で常に世界トップレベル。秘密は：①宿題が少ない ②テストが少ない ③先生の社会的地位が非常に高い ④遊びを大切にする。

Finland consistently tops global education rankings. Their secrets: less homework, fewer tests, highly respected teachers, and plenty of play time — students get 15-minute outdoor breaks every hour!

フィンランドでは「全ての子どもが輝ける」という信念のもと、競争より協力を大切にしています。
