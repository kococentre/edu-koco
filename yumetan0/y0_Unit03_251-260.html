📖 Story
文法：過去形・名詞・動詞

Hana climbed Mt. Fuji with her father.

"I need water," she said near the top.

She looked up and saw — a small store!

"Did they build this here?!" she asked.

"We love this country," her father laughed.

They went in to catch their breath.

You could drink hot cocoa and even cook instant ramen!

"Keep moving or you will get cold," said the staff.

Hana send a photo to her friends. "You will not believe this."

"Change of plan — I am staying up here forever."

🗻 CLIL豆知識：富士山の豆知識
富士山は標高3,776mで日本最高峰。世界文化遺産にも登録されています。毎年約23万人が登山し、山頂には気象観測所・郵便局・山小屋があります！

Mt. Fuji stands 3,776m tall and is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. About 230,000 people climb it each year — and yes, there really are lodges, a post office, and even shops near the summit!

山頂から出した手紙には「富士山頂郵便局」の消印が押されます。世界で一番高い場所から手紙を送れる国——それが日本！
